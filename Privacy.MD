## Privacy policy

  Marginalia — Study Notes & Highlights
  Effective date: September 12, 2026
  Publisher: Sanah Rajesh
  Contact: leapon.outreach@gmail.com

  ### Information we process

  Marginalia processes and stores information needed to provide its reading and annotation features:

  - Annotations: highlighted text, up to 40 surrounding characters on each side, notes, labels, colors, annotation
    types, saved positions, and creation or modification timestamps.

  - Reading material: imported PDFs and content extracted from EPUB, HTML, text, and Markdown files.
  - Source information: document titles, authors, filenames, website URLs—including query parameters—and timestamps for
    opening saved readings.

  - Organization: folder names, colors, and the readings or annotations assigned to them.

  Selected content and imported files may contain personal or sensitive information. Marginalia does not require an
  account or request access to your browser’s history database, cookies, contacts, or location.

  ### How information is used

  This information is used to display readings, save and restore annotations, organize material, search notes, return to
  saved positions, and export or import backups.

  Marginalia does not use your reading material or annotations for advertising, profiling, AI model training,
  creditworthiness, lending, or unrelated purposes.

  ### Local storage

  Readings, annotations, folders, and related metadata are stored in your browser profile using local extension storage.
  Original PDF files are stored in IndexedDB.

  Marginalia has no developer-operated data server, analytics service, advertising integration, or automatic cloud
  synchronization. The extension does not automatically upload your library to the publisher.

  The separate development preview uses localStorage and IndexedDB and does not automatically share its library with the
  installed extension.

  ### Website access and visibility of notes

  On permitted HTTP and HTTPS websites, Marginalia checks the current URL against saved readings and processes page text
  to restore existing highlights. When the annotation panel is enabled, it processes selected text to create
  annotations. It also responds to page changes so annotations can be repositioned.

  Marginalia does not save a general list of visited websites. Website sources and annotations are saved when you choose
  to save them.

  The on-page annotation panel is not isolated from the website. Website scripts can read notes, labels, and other
  content placed in that panel, including content that remains in the page when the panel is hidden. A website may
  collect or transmit information it can access according to its own practices. Do not enter passwords, financial
  details, or other confidential information into the on-page panel.

  ### External services and support

  Opening a source link causes your browser to contact that website. The destination’s privacy policy applies to that
  visit.

  If you email support, the publisher receives your email address and the information you send. Your email provider also
  processes that communication. Do not send private passages, credentials, or complete backups unless you intend to
  share them.

  Marginalia does not sell your data. Its JavaScript, fonts, PDF workers, and WebAssembly resources are included in the
  extension package rather than loaded as executable code from external servers.

  ### Backups and security

  Export creates an unencrypted backup file containing your saved readings, annotations, labels, folders, and available
  PDF files. Import reads the backup you select and merges its contents into your local library.

  Marginalia does not provide its own encryption or password protection for local records or backups. People or software
  with access to your device, browser profile, or exported files may be able to read them. Protect your device and store
  backups securely.

  ### Retention and deletion

  Local records remain until you delete them or your browser removes the extension’s data. You can edit or delete
  individual annotations, remove folders without deleting their contents, or use Settings & backup → Delete all local
  reading data to clear the library and imported PDF files.

  Deleting local data does not delete previously exported backups or information you have shared with websites or
  support. Uninstalling the extension or clearing browser storage may remove your library. The publisher cannot remotely
  access or delete your local records.

  ### Limited use

  Marginalia limits its use of user data to its disclosed reading and annotation features, consistent with the Chrome
  Web Store Limited Use requirements. Data is not used or transferred for advertising, unrelated monetization,
  creditworthiness, or lending.

  ### Changes and contact

  This policy will be updated when the extension’s data practices change. Material changes will be communicated where
  required.

  For privacy questions, contact Sanah Rajesh at leapon.outreach@gmail.com.
